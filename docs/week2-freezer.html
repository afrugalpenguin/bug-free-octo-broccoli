The week

Sunday

Slow Roast Pork Shoulder Serves 4

Low and slow, in early. Wednesday's fried rice comes out of the leftovers.

  • 2kg pork shoulder, skin scored
  • Sunday's share of the pork
  • 800g potatoes for roasting
  • 500g carrots
  • 1 head broccoli
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Apple sauce
  • Gravy
  1. Oven 220C for 30 minutes to start the crackling, then down to 150C for 4-5 hours.
  2. Potatoes and veg in for the last hour, oven back up to 200C for the final 20 minutes.
  3. Rest 20 minutes. Pull or slice.
  4. Set aside ~300g diced for Wednesday's fried rice before it all disappears.

~720 kcal · 44g protein · 54g carbs · 38g fat · 12g fibre

~435 kcal · 38g protein · 54g carbs · 9g fat · 2g fibre

Wednesday

Pork Fried Rice Serves 4

Freezer version. Make the lot at the first sitting, microwave per person after.

  • 300g leftover pork, diced
  • 2 rice pouches
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic cube
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Spring onions, sliced
  • 300g sweetcorn (from freezer)
  • 200g edamame (from freezer)
  • 100g peas (from freezer)
  1. Pork crisped in a hot pan, 3 minutes.
  2. Frozen veg and garlic in, 4 minutes.
  3. Rice pouches broken in, soy over, tossed 3 minutes.
  4. Eggs scrambled into a gap in the pan, then stirred through.
  5. Sesame oil off the heat. Spring onions on top.

~590 kcal · 32g protein · 63g carbs · 25g fat · 7g fibre

Saturday

Glazed Basa & Salmon Traybake Serves 4

Freezer version. Basa cooks faster than salmon, so it goes in later.

  • 4 basa fillets (from freezer)
  • 1 salmon fillet (from freezer)
  • 300g mini corn cobs (from freezer)
  • 500g baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 head broccoli, in florets
  • 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Dried herbs
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce (glaze)
  • 1 tbsp honey (glaze)
  • 1 lemon
  • Fresh dill or parsley
  1. Potatoes and mini corn cobs in first, 200C, 20 minutes.
  2. Broccoli and tomatoes in, glaze whisked and brushed over the fish.
  3. Fish on top. Basa needs 12-15 minutes, not the 15-20 salmon wants - watch it, it goes from done to dry quickly.
  4. Herbs and lemon over at the table.

~405 kcal · 33g protein · 33g carbs · 17g fat · 8g fibre

Cinnamon & Raisin Flapjacks Serves 12

First thing in the oven at 180C.

5Batch pot

On the hob, simmering through all of the oven work.

Dal Serves 8

Mild base. The tarka is an adults-only pour-over at the table.

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 300g frozen diced onion
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed
  • Thumb of fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 500g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tin light coconut milk
  • 1.2L vegetable stock
  • 100g frozen spinach
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Juice of half a lemon
  1. Onion, garlic and ginger in oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Turmeric and cumin for 1 minute.
  3. Lentils, chickpeas, tomatoes, coconut milk and stock in. Simmer 30 minutes.
  4. Spinach and garam masala for the last 5 minutes.
  5. Lemon juice at the end. Salt to taste.

With rice pouches and a spoon of yoghurt.

~315 kcal · 21g protein · 44g carbs · 9g fat · 15g fibre
