Use up week

The week

Friday to Thursday, anchored to delivery day. One prep session on Saturday night produces almost everything.

  • WednesdayOrder online. Open the basket, add to the trolley, book the slot.
  • Thursday, bedPizza dough out of the freezer into the fridge.
  • FridayDelivery. Pizza night - every week, no exceptions.
  • SaturdaySandwiches for lunch. Prep session in the evening, 2-3 hours. That night's dinner is the traybake, cooked during the session.
  • SundayTuna pasta salad for lunch, made Saturday. Roast for dinner.
  • MondayLeftovers from the roast, build-your-own. Together night.
  • Tue-ThuShift dinners - reheats, slow cooker, oven timers. Nothing over 15 minutes' active effort.
  • Wednesday, bedThursday's freezer bag out of the freezer into the fridge.

5Batch pot

On the hob, simmering through all of the oven work.

Mild Chilli, Double Batch Serves 12

Nothing hot goes in the pot. Flakes at the table for the adults.

  • 1kg 5% fat beef mince
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 300g frozen diced onion
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 1 large carrot, grated
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp tomato puree
  • 2 tins chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tins kidney beans, drained
  • 150g red lentils
  • 600ml beef stock (2 cubes)
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 15g dark chocolate (70%+)
  1. Brown the mince in the biggest pot, breaking it up. Drain any excess fat.
  2. Onion, garlic, pepper and carrot in for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Spices and tomato puree for 1 minute, stirring.
  4. Everything else in except the chocolate. Simmer 50-60 minutes, lid half off, stirring occasionally.
  5. Chocolate in at the very end, stir until melted. Salt to taste.

With rice pouches and Greek yoghurt.

Adults: Chilli flakes or hot sauce at the table.

~235 kcal · 27g protein · 20g carbs · 6g fat · 7g fibre per portion

Pulled Chicken Freezer Bag, make 2 Serves 4

Cycle 1: make two of these. One is Thursday, one goes straight into the bank.

Everything raw into the bag. Freeze flat. Wednesday night into the fridge, Thursday morning into the slow cooker on low.

  • 6 boneless chicken thighs (~600g)
  • 150g frozen onion
  • 2 garlic cubes
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp tomato puree
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 200ml passata
  • 1 chicken stock cube, crumbled
  1. Everything into a freezer bag, raw. Squeeze the air out, seal, label PULLED CHICKEN + the month.
  2. Freeze flat so it stacks.
  3. Wednesday bedtime: bag out of the freezer into the fridge.

North African Chickpea & Sweet Potato Stew Serves 8

No harissa in the pot - it goes into the adults' bowls at the table.

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 300g frozen diced onion
