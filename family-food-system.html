Freezer slabs

  • Large food bags, four portions, one bag = one dinner.
  • Squeeze air out, freeze flat on a tray, even thickness.
  • File vertically. Label the top edge: CHILLI - SEPT.
  • Pull from the front. Best inside 3 months, fine to 6.
  • Always thaw overnight in the fridge first.

The doubling rule

Every week banks at least one slab. The freezer bank compounds and insures the system: a cancelled Saturday costs nothing.

Doesn't freeze

Potatoes, cooked rice and pasta, cream. Freeze the sauce; add pouches, pasta or spuds on the day.

Cook mild, spice at the table. Every base in every recipe is mild. Heat is an adult condiment: flakes on the table, harissa or chipotle stirred through grown-up portions, the hotter paste into two bowls out of four. The boys' chilli tolerance builds slowly on their terms.

Friday pizza night 15 min

Boys' margherita

2 thawed dough balls (~250g each) · 200g passata · pinch salt · 1 tsp dried oregano · 2 balls mozzarella (125g each), torn and drained on kitchen roll · 1 tbsp olive oil
~1000 kcal · 43g protein · 126g carbs · 33g fat · 7g fibre (per whole pizza, one 250g dough ball; the boys rarely finish a whole one, so a realistic child's share is nearer 600)
  1. Oven as hot as it goes (250C+), a flat baking tray in there heating for 15 minutes.
  2. Stretch dough on floured baking paper. Thin middle, fat edge.
  3. Passata mixed with salt and oregano, thin layer, then mozzarella.
  4. Slide paper onto the hot tray. 8-10 minutes until blistered.

Adults' protein pizza

Same dough, same tray, same oven, so it costs nothing extra in effort. One more dough ball, a proper protein topping instead of just cheese, and a lighter hand with the mozzarella so it doesn't just become margherita-plus-meat. The specific topping rotates with the week and is on that week's Fri tab. Base method:

1 thawed dough ball · thin layer of passata · that week's topping (Fri tab) · mozzarella, used a little more sparingly than the boys' pizza · olive oil
  1. Stretch, sauce, scatter the week's topping over the sauce so it sits under the cheese and crisps properly, then mozzarella.
  2. Same hot tray, 8-10 minutes. Slice, finish with whatever the week's recipe calls for, split between the two of you.

The Friday salad

Rotates with the menu so pizza night never goes stale: each week's salad recipe is on that week's Fri tab. Standing rules: toasted seeds or nuts on every salad, dressing from the jar, boiled eggs come from last Saturday's dozen (week one only: boil 4 while the pizza oven heats).

Shake bags ×7 Saturday, 10 min
Per bag: 2 handfuls frozen spinach (~100g) · 100g frozen berries or half a banana · 30g oats · 1 tbsp seeds or peanut butter
~510 kcal · 43g protein · 45g carbs · 14g fat · 10g fibre (per shake, bag blended with 30g protein powder and 300ml semi-skimmed milk)
  1. Line up 7 bags, fill production-line style, back in the freezer standing in a tub.
  2. On the morning: bag into blender, 30g protein powder, ~300ml milk, blitz. Thirty seconds.
Lunch jars ×5 Saturday, 15 min
Layer order, bottom to top: 2 tbsp dressing · half a grain pouch (125g) + 50g beans · 80g of this week's protein + 100g roast veg · pickled onions · a handful of leaves
~660 kcal · 32g protein · 48g carbs · 34g fat · 9g fibre (per jar, vinaigrette weeks; week 2's yoghurt-dressed jar is nearer 500 kcal and 14g fat, since 2 tbsp of vinaigrette is 190 kcal on its own)
  1. Five jars, built in layer order so nothing goes soggy. The week's protein is on each week's Menu tab.
  2. Fridge. Holds 4-5 days. Tip into a bowl to eat.
A dozen boiled eggs Saturday, hands-off
12 eggs
~78 kcal · 7g protein · 0.4g carbs · 5g fat · 0g fibre (per large egg)
  1. Big pan, boiling water, 8 minutes, straight into cold water. Fridge in their shells, they keep a week.
  2. Standing destinations, decided forever: 4 quartered into next Friday's big salad, a few sliced into the lunch jars, the rest live next to the flapjacks as the grab-snack shelf.
Quick-pickled red onions Saturday, 10 min
2 red onions, finely sliced · 150ml white or cider vinegar · 150ml water · 1 tbsp sugar · 1 tsp salt
~10 kcal · negligible protein/fat · 2g carbs (per tbsp)
  1. Warm vinegar, water, sugar and salt until dissolved.
  2. Pour over the onions in a jar. Fridge. Ready in an hour, keeps 3 weeks, goes on everything.
Two dressings Saturday, 5 min

House vinaigrette

6 tbsp olive oil · 2 tbsp vinegar · 1 tsp mustard · 1 tsp honey · salt
~90 kcal · 0g protein · 0.6g carbs · 10g fat (per tbsp, jar makes about 8)

Yoghurt dressing

150g Greek yoghurt · juice of half a lemon · 1 garlic cube (or mint in curry week) · salt
~15 kcal · 1.2g protein · 0.5g carbs · 0.7g fat (per tbsp, jar makes about 11)

Both: everything in a jar, lid on, shake. Fridge all week.

Protein flapjacks Saturday, in the hot oven
250g oats · 60g protein powder · 150g peanut butter · 100g honey · 75g butter · 50g seeds · 1 mashed banana · pinch salt
~280 kcal · 10g protein · 24g carbs · 15g fat · 3g fibre (per bar, 1/12; whole tin ~3,330 kcal and 124g protein)
  1. Melt butter, peanut butter and honey in a big saucepan on low.
  2. Off the heat, stir in everything else. Splash of milk if dry.
  3. Press HARD into a lined 20cm-ish tin. Bake 15-18 min at 180C until golden at the edges.
  4. Cool completely in the tin before cutting into 12. Airtight for a week.

Rotate with the week: 1 classic · 2 add 1 tsp cinnamon + 50g raisins · 3 add lemon zest + extra seeds · 4 swap 20g oats for 20g cocoa.

Mild family chilli, double batch

Cooked Sat · 12 portions
1kg mince · 300g frozen diced onion · 4 garlic cubes · 2 tsp smoked paprika · 2 tsp cumin · 1 tsp oregano · 2 tbsp tomato puree · 2 tins chopped tomatoes · 2 tins kidney beans · 150g red lentils · 600ml stock
~460 kcal · 32g protein · 49g carbs · 13g fat · 6g fibre (Tuesday plate: 1/12 of the pot + half a rice pouch + 2 tbsp yoghurt. Whole pot ~2,780 kcal and 292g protein, so a bare portion of chilli is ~230 kcal and 24g protein)
  1. Saturday: brown the mince in your biggest pot. Onion and garlic in, 3 min.
  2. Spices and puree, 1 min. Everything else in. Simmer 40 min, lid half on. No heat in the pot, ever.
  3. Decant: tub for Tue, tub for Wed, one slab banked ("CHILLI - month").
  4. Tuesday: reheat + 2 rice pouches, yoghurt on top, each shift serves itself. Flakes at the table.

Double roast chicken

Serves 4 + the week
2 whole chickens · oil, salt · whatever veg is left for the roasting tray · gravy granules
~540 kcal · 51g protein · 22g carbs · 27g fat · 3g fibre (per portion, 1/4 of bird one, with roast veg and gravy)
  1. Oven 180C fan. Rub birds with oil and salt. Roast ~90 min until juices run clear (75C in the thigh).
