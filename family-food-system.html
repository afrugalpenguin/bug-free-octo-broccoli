Dinners

Four weeks of dinners on a rota, plus the things we make every week whatever week it is.

HOW IT GOES: order Wednesday → delivery Friday evening → cook session Saturday night → roast Sunday → Monday to Thursday is mostly reheating.

10 sec

Freezing a batch

  • Large food bags, four portions each, so one bag is one dinner.
  • Squeeze the air out and freeze flat on a tray, roughly even thickness.
  • Stand them upright in a tub. Write on the top edge: CHILLI - SEPT.
  • Take from the front. Best within 3 months, still fine at 6.
  • Thaw overnight in the fridge, never from frozen.

Cooking double

Saturday makes enough for at least one extra dinner. After a month or two there's enough in the freezer to cover a week we don't get round to cooking.

Doesn't freeze

Potatoes, cooked rice and pasta, cream. Freeze the sauce on its own and add the pouches, pasta or spuds on the day.

Everything is cooked mild. Chilli flakes stay on the table, harissa or chipotle goes into our two bowls at the end, and the hotter curry paste only into the adults' portions. The boys can have more heat when they ask for it.

Friday pizza night 15 min

Boys' margherita

2 thawed dough balls (~250g each) · 200g passata · pinch salt · 1 tsp dried oregano · 2 balls mozzarella (125g each), torn and drained on kitchen roll · 1 tbsp olive oil
~1000 kcal · 43g protein · 126g carbs · 33g fat · 7g fibre (per whole pizza, one 250g dough ball; the boys rarely finish a whole one, so a realistic child's share is nearer 600)
  1. Oven as hot as it goes (250C+), a flat baking tray in there heating for 15 minutes.
  2. Stretch dough on floured baking paper. Thin middle, fat edge.
  3. Passata mixed with salt and oregano, thin layer, then mozzarella.
  4. Slide paper onto the hot tray. 8-10 minutes until blistered.

Our pizza

One extra dough ball, going in the same oven on the same tray. Meat or fish on top rather than just cheese, and go easier on the mozzarella than the boys' one. The topping changes with the week and is on that week's Fri tab. The method is the same every time:

1 thawed dough ball · thin layer of passata · that week's topping (Fri tab) · mozzarella, less than on the boys' pizza · olive oil
  1. Stretch, sauce, then the topping under the cheese so it crisps rather than steams. Mozzarella last.
  2. Same hot tray, 8-10 minutes. Slice, add whatever the week's recipe finishes with, half each.

Friday salad

A different one each week, on that week's Fri tab. Toasted seeds or nuts go on all of them, the dressing comes out of the jar, and the boiled eggs come from last Saturday's dozen. First week through, boil four while the oven heats.

Sunday tuna pasta salad Saturday session, 15 min
250g pasta shapes · 2 tins tuna in spring water, drained · 2 boiled eggs, quartered · half a cucumber, diced small · 100g tomatoes, halved · 100g frozen peas, thawed under the hot tap · 2 tbsp toasted seeds · 2 tbsp Greek yoghurt · juice of half a lemon · salt, pepper
~405 kcal · 33g protein · 49g carbs · 7g fat · 4g fibre (per portion, 1/4; Sunday lunch for four)
  1. During the session, while something else is in the oven: pasta on, cooked, rinsed cold, drained well. If it goes in warm or wet it turns claggy overnight.
  2. Tuna, eggs, cucumber, tomatoes and peas through the cold pasta, then lemon, salt and pepper. Tub it and fridge it.
  3. Sunday: 2 tbsp of Greek yoghurt stirred through to loosen it, seeds over the top. Yoghurt rather than mayonnaise, which one of them won't touch.
  4. Leave the yoghurt until the day. Stir it in on Saturday and the pasta soaks it all up overnight.

Kept light, there's a roast coming that evening.

A dozen boiled eggs Saturday, hands-off
12 eggs
~78 kcal · 7g protein · 0.4g carbs · 5g fat · 0g fibre (per large egg)
  1. Big pan, boiling water, 8 minutes, then straight into cold water. Keep them in their shells in the fridge and they'll last the week.
  2. Where they end up: 4 quartered into Friday's salad, 2 into Sunday's pasta salad, a few sliced into the lunch boxes, and the rest sit next to the flapjacks for whoever wants one.
Quick-pickled red onions Saturday, 10 min
2 red onions, finely sliced · 150ml white or cider vinegar · 150ml water · 1 tbsp sugar · 1 tsp salt
~10 kcal · negligible protein/fat · 2g carbs (per tbsp)
  1. Warm vinegar, water, sugar and salt until dissolved.
  2. Pour over the onions in a jar and put it in the fridge. Ready in an hour and keeps about 3 weeks.
Two dressings Saturday, 5 min

House vinaigrette

6 tbsp olive oil · 2 tbsp vinegar · 1 tsp mustard · 1 tsp honey · salt
~90 kcal · 0g protein · 0.6g carbs · 10g fat (per tbsp, jar makes about 8)

Yoghurt dressing

150g Greek yoghurt · juice of half a lemon · 1 garlic cube (or mint in curry week) · salt
~15 kcal · 1.2g protein · 0.5g carbs · 0.7g fat (per tbsp, jar makes about 11)

Both the same: everything in a jar, lid on, shake it. They'll sit in the fridge all week.

Protein flapjacks Saturday, in the hot oven
250g oats · 60g protein powder · 150g peanut butter · 100g honey · 75g butter · 50g seeds · 1 mashed banana · pinch salt
~280 kcal · 10g protein · 24g carbs · 15g fat · 3g fibre (per bar, 1/12; whole tin ~3,330 kcal and 124g protein)
  1. Melt the butter, peanut butter and honey in a big saucepan on a low heat.
  2. Take it off the heat and stir everything else in. A splash of milk if it looks dry.
  3. Press it hard into a lined 20cm-ish tin, harder than feels necessary. 15-18 min at 180C until the edges go golden.
  4. Let it cool all the way in the tin before cutting into 12. Airtight tub, about a week.

One version per week: 1 plain · 2 with 1 tsp cinnamon and 50g raisins · 3 with lemon zest and extra seeds · 4 with 20g of the oats swapped for cocoa.

Mild chilli, double batch

Tomato and chickpea stew with couscous

Cooked Saturday · 8 portions
2 tins chickpeas · 1 tin cannellini · 200g frozen onion · 3 garlic cubes · 2 tsp cumin · 2 tsp smoked paprika · 2 tins chopped tomatoes · 2 courgettes, chunks · 500ml stock · frozen spinach at the end
~360 kcal · 15g protein · 55g carbs · 6g fat · 10g fibre (Tuesday plate: 1/8 of the pot + 50g dry couscous. Whole pot ~1,460 kcal and 69g protein, so a bare portion of stew is ~180 kcal and 9g protein)
  1. Saturday: onion and garlic in oil for 3 min, spices for another minute, then everything else. Simmer 25 min, spinach in for the last 5. Salt.
  2. A tub for Tuesday, one bag frozen and labelled CHICKPEA STEW with the month.
  3. Tuesday: reheat and let each sitting help itself. For the couscous, 200g in a bowl with 250ml boiling stock over it, a plate on top for 5 min, then fork it through.
  4. Harissa stirred through our two bowls.

The big ragu, with fresh pasta

Cooked Saturday · Monday, Tuesday and the freezer
1kg mince, 5% fat · 300g frozen onion · 4 garlic cubes · 2 carrots, grated · 2 tbsp puree · 2 tins chopped tomatoes · 300ml passata · 2 tsp oregano · 400ml stock. On the night: 500g fresh pasta and parmesan
~585 kcal · 40g protein · 71g carbs · 15g fat · 5g fibre (Tuesday plate: 1/4 of the Tuesday third + 125g fresh pasta and parmesan. The whole ragu is ~1,930 kcal and 228g protein, split three ways)
  1. Saturday: brown the mince properly, don't rush it. Onion, garlic and carrot for 4 min, then the puree and everything else. Simmer 45 min with the lid half on.
  2. Split it three ways: a third into the lasagne (Mon tab), a tub for Tuesday, and one bag frozen and labelled RAGU with the month.
  3. Tuesday: reheat the tub while the fresh pasta cooks, which takes about 3 min. Parmesan over the top.

  2. Beef in for the last 2 minutes, just to warm through. Parmesan over the top.

Minestrone and garlic bread
